# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Device Guard welcomes contributors, operators, security reviewers, educators,
and Android developers. We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free
community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or
ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while discussing ideas, but accept correction when an
  interaction causes harm.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on evidence, trade-offs, and user safety.
- Protect private information. Never publish device credentials, administrator
  PINs, recovery material, customer data, or unredacted logs.
- Respect the project's safety boundary: Device Owner and kiosk changes can
  make devices difficult to recover, so risky instructions require clear
  warnings and reproducible evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
doxxing, sustained disruption, and deliberate disclosure of private or
security-sensitive information are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and may temporarily
or permanently restrict participation when behavior is unsafe or abusive.
Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented privately, and avoid
unnecessary disclosure about reporters or affected people.

For an ordinary project question, use
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/ShalomMaman/android-lockdown-dpc/discussions).
For a confidential conduct concern, contact the maintainers through
[GitHub private reporting](https://github.com/ShalomMaman/android-lockdown-dpc/security/advisories/new)
and begin the report title with `Conduct report:`. Do not use a public Issue.

## Scope

This code applies in repository Issues, Pull Requests, Discussions, Releases,
and other public spaces where someone represents the Device Guard project.

This policy is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
and the [GitHub Community Guidelines](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines).
